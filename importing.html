Importing your live account data

Using AlecaFrame

If you are using AlecaFrame, your encrypted inventory is already stored on your machine. You can use this tool to help you locate and decrypt it.

Using warframe-api-helper

Download warframe-api-helper to get your credentials while the game is running and logged in on live. It will download your inventory for you and store it in inventory.json.

Using the credentials it prints (?accountId=...&nonce=...), it is also possible to get your personal rooms: https://api.warframe.com/api/getShip.php?accountId=...&nonce=...

Closing the game will invalidate these credentials.

Importing to your server

Now that you have your inventory, open the WebUI and click on Import. Paste the content of your inventory and click Submit.

If you have a getShip response, you can import it the same way. Otherwise, use Unlock All Ship Features in the Cheats tab to avoid being locked out of most things.
